# Executive Insight Design System Extension (`DESIGN.md`)

> **Extends**: `company/DESIGN.md`  
> **Target Domain**: Board-level summaries, executive KPI readouts, strategic dashboards, investor reporting.

## 1. Domain Principles
* **Executive Clarity**: Large metric displays with narrative context, trend indicators ($\uparrow \downarrow$), and variance analysis.
* **Low Density, High Hierarchy**: Generous spatial rhythm (`24px`-`32px` gaps) to focus attention on key metrics.
* **Export-Ready Layouts**: Views formatted for clean PDF export and slide presentations.
