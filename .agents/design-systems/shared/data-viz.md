# Shared Data Visualization Standards (`data-viz.md`)

1. **Color Palette**: Use distinct, accessible chart colors (`#2563eb`, `#0d9488`, `#d97706`, `#7c3aed`, `#db2777`).
2. **Chart Accessibility**: Colors must not be the sole indicator of meaning; use patterns, markers, or labels.
3. **Tooltip & Legends**: Tooltips must display exact values, units, and timestamps.
