# Shared Microcopy & Content Voice Standards (`content-voice.md`)

1. **Tone**: Concise, active, objective. Avoid fluff or casual conversational filler.
2. **Action Labels**: Buttons must use active verb phrases (e.g., "Save Changes", "Submit Order", "Export CSV").
3. **Error Messages**: Always explain (1) what happened, (2) why, and (3) how to resolve it.
