# Binance Mobile App — 20-Screen Complete Design Brief

> **Feature ID**: `024-binance-mobile-20-screens`  
> **Domain**: Mobile Fintech & Crypto Exchange  
> **Design System**: `binance/DESIGN.md` (Binance.US Inspired)  
> **Screen Count**: **20 Mobile Screens** (`MOB-001` to `MOB-020`) + 1 System Specs Artboard  
> **Target Device**: iPhone 15 Pro ($390 \times 844\text{px}$)  

---

## 1. Problem Framing & Core Goal

* **Business Problem**: Mobile crypto traders need a seamless 20-screen journey covering onboarding, auth, KYC, spot trading, futures, P2P, earn vaults, and wallet management without visual drop-offs.
* **Target Outcome**: Build a 20-screen mobile design flow on an interactive Figma canvas board where traders can interact with every single step.
* **Key Constraints**:
  - Bound to `binance/DESIGN.md`.
  - Binance Yellow (`#F0B90B`) primary accent.
  - Crypto Green (`#0ECB81`) and Crypto Red (`#F6465D`) status indicators.
  - $50\text{px}$ pill-shaped touch buttons.

---

## 2. 20 Mobile Screens Scope

1. **`MOB-001` — Splash & Brand Welcome**: Golden Binance logo & welcome screen.
2. **`MOB-002` — Onboarding 1**: Security & Cold Storage guarantee.
3. **`MOB-003` — Onboarding 2**: Instant Spot & Futures Trading.
4. **`MOB-004` — Auth Login**: Biometric FaceID & Password Login.
5. **`MOB-005` — 2FA Security**: SMS / Google Authenticator OTP code input.
6. **`MOB-006` — KYC Verification**: Identity document camera photo upload.
7. **`MOB-007` — Home Market Dashboard**: Market cap summary, hot gainers, and banner slider.
8. **`MOB-008` — Search & Watchlist**: Live search with favorite crypto tickers.
9. **`MOB-009` — BTC/USDT Spot Chart Viewport**: Candlestick chart & timeframes.
10. **`MOB-010` — Depth Orderbook & Trade Drawer**: Live Bids/Asks depth & market buy drawer.
11. **`MOB-011` — Buy Crypto Fiat Gateway**: Credit card & Apple Pay instant purchase.
12. **`MOB-012` — Convert & Swap**: Zero-fee instant crypto swap interface.
13. **`MOB-013` — Futures Trading**: 125x leverage selector & position liquidation margin.
14. **`MOB-014` — Binance Earn**: Flexible & Locked APY staking vaults.
15. **`MOB-015` — Spot Wallet Overview**: Asset allocation pie chart & total balance.
16. **`MOB-016` — Deposit Crypto QR Scanner**: Receive BTC/USDT wallet QR code.
17. **`MOB-017` — Withdraw Crypto**: External address & network fee check.
18. **`MOB-018` — P2P Merchant Express**: Peer-to-peer bank transfer merchant list.
19. **`MOB-019` — Transaction History**: Exportable trade logs & tax statement summary.
20. **`MOB-020` — Account Security Settings**: Anti-phishing code, API keys, and FaceID toggles.
