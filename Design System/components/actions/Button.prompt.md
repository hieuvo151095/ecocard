The ECO primary action control — use it for every tap target that commits an action; `soil` is the yellow brand button, `outline` and `plain` are the secondary and tertiary steps down.

```jsx
<Button type="soil" size="large" onClick={submit}>Tiếp tục</Button>
<Button type="outline" size="medium">Huỷ</Button>
<Button type="plain" size="small" contentType="leading icon" leadingIcon={<Icon name="ASearchProperty1Line" size={16} />}>Tìm</Button>
```

Variants: `type` soil | outline | plain · `size` small (32px) | medium (40px) | large (48px) · `shape` default/border (8px radius) | round (30px pill) · `contentType` text only | leading icon | trailing icon | icon only · `disabled` (5% black wash at 60% opacity) · `pressed` (#FECD4D).
